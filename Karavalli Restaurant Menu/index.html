The Soul of Karavalli

For decades, Karavalli has been dedicated to honoring the indigenous recipes of the coastal belt. We source fragrant tellicherry peppercorns, cold-pressed coconut oils, clay-baked earthen pots, and traditional tiffin griddles.

Every dish—from our slow-simmered biriyanis to our crisp golden dosas and house-churned artisanal kulfis—is an ode to coastal culinary craftsmanship.
